We tested the Webz.io News API and NewsData.io against the same topics, countries, languages, search operators, and publication window. The result was consistent: Webz.io returned substantially more matching news, delivered full article text in every sampled record, and responded faster. Across the aligned 48-hour window, Webz.io reported 5,513,536 matching articles, compared with 929,581 from NewsData.io.
